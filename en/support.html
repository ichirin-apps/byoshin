Byoshin shows the seconds using iOS Live Activities. Some things are set by iOS and can’t be changed by apps, so we’ve answered common questions here.

How do I keep the seconds on all day?

Use Shortcuts automations to restart the seconds display every 6 hours (a Byoshin Pro feature). Always-On Setup in the app walks you through it one screen at a time, matched to your version of iOS.

  • In Shortcuts, tap Automation → + → Time of Day
  • Set the time to 12:00 AM, repeat Daily, and choose Run Immediately
  • Add the action “Start Seconds Display” (search for “Byoshin”)
  • Create the same automation for 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM (4 in total)

How can I check that it’s set up?

Tap “Set Up Always-On n/4” on the main screen to see the status of each of the 4 times. A ✓ appears once the automation has run after its scheduled time.

The seconds don’t appear

Please check that Settings → Byoshin → Live Activities is turned on. When it’s off, iOS doesn’t allow the display, so the seconds can’t be shown.

After turning it on, open Byoshin and tap Start.

The display disappears after about 8 hours

On iOS, a Live Activity can stay on screen for up to 8 hours at a time, so every app’s Live Activity ends after about 8 hours.

If you create the automations in Always-On Setup, the display restarts automatically every 6 hours and the seconds stay on all day (a Byoshin Pro feature). Without automations, you can tap the notification that arrives shortly before the end to restart the display.

Can I hide it on the Lock Screen only?

On iOS, the Dynamic Island and the Lock Screen Live Activity come as a set, and an app can’t show only one of them.

To make up for this, Byoshin keeps its Lock Screen display to a single, slim line that stays out of the way of your wallpaper.

I swiped it away on the Lock Screen and the seconds stopped

When you swipe a Live Activity away on the Lock Screen, iOS ends it. It won’t come back until the next automation runs.

To bring it back right away, open Byoshin and tap Start, or use the widget or the Control Center button.

With another app’s Live Activity, the seconds get smaller or disappear

When there are two or more Live Activities, iOS decides automatically which one is shown large and which one small (as just a round icon). Apps can’t choose the order.

Navigation, timers and similar activities may take priority while they’re running, but once the other app’s activity ends, the seconds return to their usual size.

The seconds aren’t in the Dynamic Island while Byoshin is open

On iOS, an app’s own Live Activity isn’t shown in the Dynamic Island while that app is open.

Go to the Home Screen or switch to another app and it appears right away. While Byoshin is open, you can see the seconds in large numbers on the screen.

The automations don’t seem to run

Please check the following in the Shortcuts app:

  • Run Immediately is selected (with Run After Confirmation, the display won’t start on its own)
  • If the action shows “Open When Run”, it’s turned off
  • All 4 automations (12:00 AM, 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 6:00 PM) exist and are turned on

Always-on is a Byoshin Pro feature. Always-On Status in Byoshin shows whether each time is working (the ✓ appears after the scheduled time).

Does it work on iPhone models without the Dynamic Island?

Yes. On models without the Dynamic Island, the seconds appear as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in Notification Center. You can also see them in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (widgets are a Byoshin Pro feature).

The seconds sometimes look about a second off

The seconds are drawn by iOS from your iPhone’s clock. iOS refreshes the status bar time and Live Activities at slightly different moments, so they can look a tiny bit apart—for example, right when the minute changes.

The time itself isn’t off, so you can rely on it. iOS may also update the display less often in Low Power Mode or when the screen is dimmed.

Does it affect battery life?

iOS draws the seconds, and Byoshin doesn’t keep running in the background, so the impact is kept small.

That said, keeping a Live Activity on all day uses a little more power than not showing one. You can stop it any time with Stop in the app.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the settings button at the top right of Byoshin, then Restore Purchase. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Byoshin Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
