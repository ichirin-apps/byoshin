How do I keep the seconds on screen all the time?

Use a Shortcuts automation to restart the display every 6 hours (a Byoshin Pro feature).

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, go to Automation, and tap +
  2. Choose “Time of Day”, set the time to 12:00 AM, and choose “Daily”
  3. Select “Run Immediately”
  4. Turn off “Notify When Run”, then tap Next
  5. Choose the “Start Seconds Display” action and tap Done
  6. Repeat for 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM, and 6:00 PM (4 in total)

The key points: select “Run Immediately” and turn off “Notify When Run”.

How can I check that it's set up?

On the app's main screen, tap “Set Up Always-On n/4” to see the status of each of the 4 times (✓). If a time hasn't run yet, the app shows how to set it up. A ✓ appears after the scheduled time has passed.

Why does the display disappear after 8 hours?

iOS ends Live Activities (the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen display) after 8 hours at most. Byoshin sends a notification shortly before that; tap it to keep the display going. To keep it on all the time, set up the automation above.

The seconds don't appear

Open the Settings app → Byoshin and make sure Live Activities is turned on. On iPhone models without the Dynamic Island, you can see the seconds on the Lock Screen and in widgets.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the settings button at the top right of the app, then “Restore Purchase”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Byoshin Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Does it drain the battery?

iOS draws the seconds itself and the app doesn't keep running in the background, so the impact is small.
