Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Ichirin ("we") handles information in the iPhone app Byoshin (the "App").

Information we collect

The App does not collect any data, including personal information. It does not send data to our servers, and it uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (such as the color, weight, and font) are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive this information.

Payment

Byoshin is a paid app. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and we never receive your payment information. There are no in-app purchases.

Notifications

The reminder before the display ends is a local notification scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere for it.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
